# Proposed Screen: Travel Quote

This text description represents one proposed desktop screen. Treat the named
sections as visible design layers.

## Visible sections

- **Header:** Globe logo, "Travel insurance", Help link.
- **Hero:** "Travel with confidence" and one sentence about getting a quick quote.
- **Quote form:** Destination, departure date, return date, traveler ages, and
  an "Add adventure sports" checkbox.
- **Price card:** "Your price: ₪126", "For two travelers", and a
  "Continue to purchase" button.
- **Benefits:** Medical support, luggage assistance, and quick online service.
- **Footer:** Contact link and privacy link.

## Not visible

- Field validation messages.
- An explanation of how the price was calculated.
- A statement explaining whether the price is final.
- What happens after selecting **Continue to purchase**.
