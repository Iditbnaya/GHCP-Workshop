# Customer Request: Online Travel-Insurance Quote

A traveler wants to receive a simple travel-insurance quote before a trip.

| ID | Requested outcome |
|---|---|
| TR-01 | The traveler enters the destination, travel dates, and ages of all travelers. |
| TR-02 | The traveler sees one total premium and a simple explanation of the price. |
| TR-03 | The traveler can add adventure-sports coverage. |
| TR-04 | The traveler can continue from the quote to purchase. |
| TR-05 | Invalid information produces a clear message without removing entered information. |

## Not decided yet

- Which countries belong to each destination group.
- Why the current age bands and multipliers were selected.
- Which coverages are included in the displayed price.
- Whether trips longer than 30 days can be insured through another process.
- Which payment or policy system completes the purchase.

This is a product request, not an approved specification.
