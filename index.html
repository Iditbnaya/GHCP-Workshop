Working agreement

Evidence before confidence

Copilot accelerates the work, but the analyst remains accountable for every rule, diagram element, requirement, and design finding.

01

Cite the source

Use exact documents, files, functions, calculations, and layers.

02

Label evidence status

Separate Current, Requested, Assumed, Gap, and Open question.

03

Keep context focused

Provide only the smallest folder, file, selection, or frame needed.

04

Respect tool boundaries

Check the provider, input, output, effect, and permission of every tool.
