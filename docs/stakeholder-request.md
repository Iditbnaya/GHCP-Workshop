# Stakeholder Request: Promotions Enhancement

The sales team wants the order-pricing system to support promotional coupons in addition to membership discounts.

Requested outcomes:

- A customer can provide one coupon code with an order.
- A valid coupon reduces the order price.
- The order summary shows the membership discount and coupon discount separately.
- Tax is calculated after all applicable discounts.
- Invalid or expired coupons produce a clear message.
- Existing orders without coupons continue to behave as they do today.

The stakeholder has not yet decided:

- Which coupon types are supported.
- Whether coupons can be combined with membership discounts.
- Whether a coupon applies before or after the membership discount.
- Who creates and expires coupon codes.
- Whether there is a maximum total discount.
- What exact message is shown for invalid or expired coupons.

This document describes a business request, not an approved functional specification.
